## 6. Candle safety

A lit candle is an open flame. Burn candles on a stable, non-flammable surface, away from curtains, paper and anything flammable, and out of reach of children and pets. Never leave a burning candle unattended. Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each lighting. Beeswax can drip, so place holders on a surface that's easy to clean.

The iron candleholders are deliberately rusted. Rust can mark light-coloured or damp surfaces, so place them on a protected surface.
