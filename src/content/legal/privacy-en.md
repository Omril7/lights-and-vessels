## 1. Who We Are

The website is operated by **Alon Bar-El**, a sole trader. For any privacy-related inquiry, please write to the email address found on the "About" page.

## 2. What Information We Collect

When you place an order, you provide the Studio with the following information:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)
- Shipping address (street, city, postal code)
- Your order history on the site

In addition, technical information is automatically collected from your browser: IP address, device type, browser and language, and statistical data about the pages you visited. This data is used only for troubleshooting and improving your browsing experience.

## 3. Why We Collect It

- Order processing: payment processing, issuing a receipt, preparing and tracking your shipment.
- Communication with you about your specific order (confirmation, status updates, pickup coordination).
- Fulfilling legal obligations (income documentation, bookkeeping, tax authorities).
- Improving the site using aggregated, non-identifiable statistical data.

The Studio **does not send marketing emails** and does not share your details with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Third Parties

To operate the site and provide the service, information is shared with the following service providers, who are bound by terms ensuring data security and privacy:

- **Morning (Green Invoice)** — payment processing, issuing receipts, and tax reporting. Receives name, email, address, and order details.
- **Resend** (or Gmail as backup) — sending operational order confirmation emails. Receives name, email, and email content.
- **Supabase** — cloud storage for the database and images, within the European Union.
- **Vercel** — site hosting and request logs. Receives technical data (IP, browser, request path).
- **Google** — used solely for the Studio administrator to log in to the management panel. Customers are not required to connect a Google account.

## 5. Cookies

- **Shopping cart** — stored only in your browser (localStorage), not on the server. Allows items to be saved between visits.
- **Accessibility settings** — stored in the browser to restore your preferences on each visit.
- **Admin login cookie** — created only when logging in to the admin panel and is not relevant to customers.

The site does not use advertising cookies, does not embed social media pixels, and does not track you across other websites.

## 6. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Protection Act, you have the right to access information stored about you at the Studio, to request corrections, and to request deletion of your account and personal information not required for legal obligations. Requests can be sent to the email address on the "About" page. The Studio will respond to all such requests within 30 days.

## 7. Data Retention

Order details are stored for 7 years, in accordance with legal requirements for income documentation. After this period, data is deleted or anonymised. Technical data unrelated to orders is retained for a shorter period, according to the infrastructure providers' log policies.

## 8. Security

The site is secured with HTTPS throughout. Payments are processed on Morning's external payment page, and card details do not pass through or get stored on the Studio's servers. Access to the admin panel is limited to authorised email addresses via authenticated Google sign-in.

## 9. Minors

The site is not intended for purchases by persons under the age of 18. Placing an order constitutes a declaration that the purchaser is of legal age.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be available on the site, and the date of the last update will appear at the bottom of this page.
