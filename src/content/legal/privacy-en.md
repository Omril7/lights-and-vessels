## 1. Who is responsible

**Alon Bar-El**, exempt dealer (osek patur), the studio "Lights and Vessels". For any privacy question: {{email}}.

## 2. What we collect

**When you place an order**, to handle it:

- full name
- email address
- phone number
- shipping address, if you choose postal delivery
- order details: items, amount, delivery method

There are no user accounts on the site; you don't need to sign up to buy.

**Automatically:** our hosting provider logs basic technical information (IP address, browser type, the page requested) for security and troubleshooting. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools on the site.

## 3. What it's used for

- Handling your order: payment, receipt, shipping and updates.
- Contacting you about your order, for example to arrange pickup.
- Meeting legal obligations, such as keeping income records.

**We don't send marketing email, and we don't sell or share your information for marketing.**

## 4. Service providers that receive information

Each receives only what its service needs:

- **Morning (Green Invoice):** card payments and receipts. Receives name, email, phone and order details. Card details are entered directly with them and never pass through us.
- **An email service (Gmail or Resend):** sends your order confirmation. Receives name, email and the confirmation content.
- **Supabase:** database and image storage, on servers in the European Union.
- **Vercel:** hosts the site. Receives technical request information.
- **WhatsApp (Meta):** only if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp; that conversation is also covered by WhatsApp's own policy.

## 5. What's stored in your browser

The site stores in your browser (localStorage, not on our servers):

- your **cart**, so items stay there between visits
- your **accessibility settings** and chosen **colour theme**

There are no advertising or tracking cookies. A sign-in cookie is created only when the studio's owner signs in to the admin area.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, except information we must keep by law. Write to {{email}} and we'll answer within 30 days.

## 7. How long we keep it

Order details are kept for 7 years, as tax law requires, and then deleted. Technical information is kept briefly, under the hosting provider's policy.

## 8. Security

The site runs over HTTPS. Payment happens on Morning's secure page, and we never store card details. Access to the admin area is limited to authorised accounts.

## 9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
