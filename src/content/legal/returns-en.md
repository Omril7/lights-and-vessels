## 1. 14-Day Cancellation Right

Under the Consumer Protection Act, you may cancel an order and return the item **within 14 days of receiving your shipment** and receive a full refund to the original payment method.

Conditions for a refund: the item must be returned to the Studio **unused and in its original condition**, in original packaging with the receipt enclosed. Items that were damaged or scratched after delivery will not be eligible for a refund, though each case may be reviewed individually.

## 2. How to Cancel

To submit a cancellation request, contact the Studio at the email address on the "About" page and include:

- Order number
- Full name
- Reason for cancellation (optional, but helps us improve)

After your request is approved, you will receive the Studio's address to return the item. We recommend using a tracked delivery service, as responsibility for the item on its way back rests with the customer until it arrives at the Studio.

## 3. Return Shipping Costs

- **Change of mind** (item is fine but doesn't suit you) — the cost of return shipping to the Studio is the customer's responsibility. The original shipping fee paid is non-refundable.
- **Damaged or incorrect item** — the Studio will cover the full cost of return shipping and of sending the replacement item, at no additional charge.

## 4. Custom Orders

Items made especially for you to a personal order (Made to Order), including size adjustments, custom colours, or unique engraving, **cannot be cancelled** once preparation has begun. This is provided for under the Consumer Protection Act for goods manufactured specifically for the consumer.

However, if the item made for you does not match the order — you will be entitled to a replacement at no additional charge.

## 5. Refund

The financial refund will be processed via a credit invoice in the Morning system and returned to the credit card used for the transaction. The refund will be issued **within 14 business days** of the Studio receiving and inspecting the returned item.

The Studio does not charge **cancellation fees** as long as the cancellation is made within the 14-day cooling-off period and the item is returned in its original condition.

## 6. Non-Refundable Items

- Items made to a personal order (see section 4).
- Items that have been used, worn, or damaged after delivery.
- Items returned without original packaging or without a receipt.
- Items returned more than 14 days after receiving the shipment.

## 7. Lost Package

If your shipment has not arrived within the expected delivery time, please contact the Studio and we will open an inquiry with Israel Post. If the shipment is confirmed lost — you will receive a full refund or a replacement item of your choice. Further details in the [Shipping Policy](/legal/shipping).

## 8. Changes to Policy

The Studio may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be available on the site, and the date of the last update will appear at the bottom of this page.
