## 1. Cancelling within 14 days

You can cancel your order **within 14 days of receiving the item** and get a full refund of the item's price.

The item must come back **in its original condition and unused**, in the packaging it arrived in.

- **Candles:** we can only take back candles that haven't been lit and whose wick is intact.
- **Numbered one-of-a-kind pieces:** the right to cancel applies to them like any other item.

**Senior citizens (65 and over), people with disabilities and new immigrants** may cancel within **4 months** of receiving the item, under the Consumer Protection Law.

## 2. How to cancel

Send us a message at {{email}} or [on WhatsApp]({{whatsapp}}) with:

- your order number
- the name the order was placed under

We'll confirm and send you the return address. We recommend registered mail, because the item is the sender's responsibility until it reaches the studio.

## 3. Who pays for return shipping

- **Change of mind** (the item is fine): return shipping is on the customer, and the original shipping fee isn't refunded.
- **Damaged or wrong item:** we pay for the return and for sending a replacement, or refund the full amount including shipping.

We **don't charge a cancellation fee**.

## 4. When you get your refund

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Morning, with a credit invoice, **within 14 days** of receiving your cancellation notice.

## 5. What can't be returned

- **Items made specially to your order** (for example in a size, colour or material you chose), once we've started making them. This follows the Consumer Protection Law and its regulations. If a made-to-order piece doesn't match what we agreed, we'll fix or replace it.
- **Candles that have been lit.**
- **Items damaged** after they reached you.

## 6. Items that didn't arrive

If your parcel hasn't arrived within the expected time, contact us and we'll follow up with Israel Post. More in the [shipping policy](/legal/shipping).

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Your order is covered by the version on the site when you placed it.
