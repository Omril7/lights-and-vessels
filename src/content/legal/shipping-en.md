## 1. Delivery Methods

The Studio offers two delivery methods:

- **Israel Post (registered mail)** — to any locality in Israel.
- **Self pickup** from the Studio — by prior arrangement via WhatsApp only. The address and pickup hours will be sent after the order is confirmed.

## 2. Shipping Costs

- **Registered mail — ₪30** per order (regardless of how many items).
- **Self pickup — free.**

The current cost is shown at checkout before final confirmation. If a free-shipping promotion is active above a certain amount, this will be clearly indicated in the cart and the top banner on the site.

## 3. Processing and Delivery Times

The Studio works in small batches, so please allow for two stages:

- **Studio preparation time:** 1–3 business days from payment confirmation until the parcel is handed to the post office.
- **Israel Post delivery time:** up to 7 business days from handover to the post office, depending on destination. During busy periods (holidays, sales) this may be longer.

Total expected time end to end: **up to approximately 10 business days**. For urgent orders, please contact us via WhatsApp to check if earlier dispatch is possible.

## 4. Packaging

Every item is hand-wrapped in tissue paper and placed in a small, sturdy cardboard box. If the order is a gift, we'd be happy to include a handwritten note — mention this in the notes field at checkout.

## 5. Tracking

After the parcel is handed to the post office, you will receive an email with a **tracking number**. You can track your shipment on the Israel Post website. If there is no update for an extended period, or if the shipment has not arrived, please contact the Studio and we will open an inquiry with the post office.

## 6. Loss or Damage

Israel Post has a **window of up to 30 days** to confirm a registered parcel as lost. If the shipment is confirmed lost — you will receive either a replacement item or a full refund of your choice. In the case of damage in transit, please photograph the packaging and item immediately upon receipt and send the photos to the Studio, so we can file a claim with the post office.

## 7. Incorrect Address or Unavailable Recipient

Please make sure the address entered at checkout is correct. If a shipment is returned to the Studio due to an incorrect address, refusal to accept the parcel, or failure to collect from the post office — re-sending it to the customer will incur an additional shipping charge.

## 8. International Shipping

At this stage the Studio **does not ship outside Israel**. If you are abroad and interested in purchasing — please contact us via WhatsApp and we will explore a personal arrangement.

## 9. Cancellations and Returns

The cancellation and returns policy is detailed in the [Returns & Cancellations Policy](/legal/returns).
