## 1. Delivery options

- **Israel Post (registered mail):** to anywhere in Israel.
- **Pickup from the studio:** arranged in advance on WhatsApp. The address and pickup hours are sent after your order is confirmed.

## 2. Cost

- **Registered mail: {{post_price}}** per order, however many items it contains.
- **Pickup: free.**

## 3. Times

- **Preparing in the studio:** {{processing_days}} business days from payment until the parcel is handed to the post.
- **Postal delivery:** {{post_eta}} from handover. It can take longer in busy periods, such as holidays.

In a hurry? [Message us on WhatsApp]({{whatsapp}}) and we'll see if we can speed things up.

## 4. Packing

Candles are packed so they won't break or bend, and iron-and-stone pieces are padded. If it's a gift, we're happy to add a handwritten note. Mention it in the checkout notes or on WhatsApp.

## 5. Tracking

Once the parcel is with the post, we email you a **tracking number** that you can follow on the Israel Post website. If there's no progress for a while, contact us and we'll follow up with the post.

## 6. Loss or damage in transit

- **Damage:** photograph the packaging and the item as soon as you receive it and send us the photos. We'll send a replacement, if one exists, or refund the full amount.
- **Loss:** if the post confirms the parcel is lost, you can choose a replacement or a full refund. Israel Post can take up to 30 days to confirm the loss of registered mail.

A one-of-a-kind piece has no identical replacement, so in that case you get a full refund.

## 7. Wrong address or uncollected parcels

Please check the address at checkout. If a parcel comes back to the studio because of a wrong address, a refusal or an uncollected parcel, sending it again costs an additional shipping fee.

## 8. International shipping

For now we ship **within Israel only**. Outside Israel? [Message us]({{whatsapp}}) and we'll look into it.
